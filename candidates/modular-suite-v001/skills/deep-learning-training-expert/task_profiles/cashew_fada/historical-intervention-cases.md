# Retrieve project-owned intervention evidence

Actual Cashew observations, comparisons, numerical summaries and source hashes
belong in the explicitly authorized project experience directory, not in this
installed Skill. The earlier bundled historical Markdown and JSON were preserved
as project-owned `legacy_imports`; their import receipt is an observation, not a
new matched experiment or verification of original checkpoint bytes.

Use the caller's `project_id`, `project_root`, `experience_dir` and authorization
with the sibling `training-experience-update` helper. Do not discover an assumed
Cashew path or borrow another project's cases. Missing identity or path means no
write. Retrieve a few matching summaries; open original hashed artifacts only
when they can resolve the present uncertainty.

## Distilled mechanisms to retrieve

Use the conditional lesson index before the raw comparison archive. The
following are diagnostic distinctions to apply, not fixed tuning prescriptions.
Their actual counts, configurations, original artifacts and contradictions
remain in the authorized project's reviewed lessons.

|Current uncertainty|Retrieve mechanism|Decision-relevant distinction|
|---|---|---|
|Lower adversarial weight moved a ratio but errors persist|`adversarial_pressure`|Mechanical pressure reduction does not predict target area or quality; inspect remaining supervision and source state.|
|A weaker/stronger D appears beneficial|`discriminator_tracking`|D fit, tracking and semantic preservation are different; source and response horizon can reverse an apparent benefit.|
|Lower LR produces an unexpected gradient ratio or map change|`model_update_size`|Raw gradients do not measure adaptive-optimizer displacement; stability and semantic accuracy need separate evidence.|
|Adding negatives reduced cashew predictions|`weak_negative_supervision`|Check provenance, protected positives and actual dose; reduced area does not establish retained recall or better F1.|
|A small development panel prefers a branch or checkpoint|`selection_generalization`|Spatial support and paired errors matter; a local preference can reverse on another support. Already exposed final outcomes cannot become unseen evaluation.|
|A very short trial has nonzero gradients but unchanged F1|`trial_exposure`|Execution is established, efficacy may remain unresolved; do not declare success, universal failure or keep searching the same panel until it wins.|

Query the mechanism together with known source/component state and exposure;
read at most the few relevant lessons, including a contrary case. If no matching
experience exists, use current facts and mechanism knowledge rather than forcing
a historical analogy. These distinctions consolidate recurring issues; adding
one more raw run should not automatically add another rule to the Skill.

Match the actual changed path, start/opponent/optimizer state, training origin,
effective exposure, source and target year, decoder, label version, spatial
support and response horizon. Keep negative and null results and contradictory
tiles. Repeated branches, years and seeds within a tile are correlated. Do not
infer a target-quality effect from a gradient ratio, map fraction or source F1.

Historical automatic, supplementary and human pools retain their own origin,
protection and dose semantics. Their weights do not become current defaults.
Original sealed-test results already exposed as history cannot become an unseen
test of this revised Skill. Current dev feedback is separately versioned and
tracked; threshold selection remains a separate task.
