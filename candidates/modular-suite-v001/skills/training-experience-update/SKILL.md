# Training Experience Update

This generic Skill manages rules; actual cases belong to their corresponding authorized project. Read [project store and validation-use history](references/project-store.md) when retrieving or writing experience. Require explicit project identity, root, dedicated experience directory and write authorization. Missing or conflicting identity/path means no write. Never default to Cashew or an installed Skill directory. The helper implements project isolation, hashed evidence, immutable append-only cases and finite development-use reservations.

Improve future decisions by correcting the smallest unsupported claim. For case retrieval, cross-task reuse or a contradicted lesson, read [transferable experience and revision](references/transferable-experience-and-revision.md). Use its mechanism-based matching and positive/null/adverse lesson distinctions. It explains what TNP, Reflexion, ExpeL and A-Mem contribute and which conclusions they do not support. Use only actual permitted records and preserve prior decisions and their historical information cutoff.

The shorter [conditional experience](references/conditional-experience.md) provides an orientation, not a substitute for the detailed matching and revision methods.

Distinguish a recorded observation, a matched intervention effect and a mechanistic inference. Record the task, component and data identities, permitted metric, visible state, executed change and exposure, response horizon, alternative explanations, costs and applicable conditions. This can be concise prose under the caller's existing format; no new memory service is required.

Include no-change, null, adverse, failed and incomplete results. A scalar that moved a gradient norm without improving measured errors supplies a mechanical lesson, not a quality prescription. Different years or seeds at the same location do not create independent geographic tasks. If a later label correction invalidates a comparison, restrict or withdraw its claim without erasing the original artifact.

For empirical action-to-response knowledge, a single task remains an observation or hypothesis. Promote a reusable relationship only after the executed action repeats under comparable conditions across distinct task units. Define independence for that project; spatial projects count distinct tiles and keep correlated years/seeds/branches within their tile. State support, horizon, agreements and counterexamples. Replication of a mechanical response does not promote an accuracy claim. Historical records stay in the owning project and their numerical values do not become global defaults.

Record authorized validation feedback: confusion, Precision/Recall/F1, both confusing-class error directions, dataset/label/checkpoint identities, decoder and use history. Keep observations, matched effects and hypotheses distinct. Feed relevant summaries into the next diagnosis/algorithm shortlist instead of accumulating unused logs. Prior feedback is not a formal threshold or systematic method study. Development permission does not authorize validation-label backpropagation or independent-test claims.

When reusing experience, explain the relevant mechanism match and meaningful differences. A similar feature distance or geography alone does not establish a shared optimal setting. Historical winners may reflect extra budget, repeated selection or information not visible at the new decision time.

Make a local knowledge update only when within scope, version it and retain provenance. Do not automatically rewrite a frozen Skill, active experiment or production caller. A proposed lesson can remain unconfirmed. Completion means a traceable conditional lesson or withdrawal, not evidence that retrieval itself improves training.
